What Is Exillion?

Everybody Has A Second Chance

Exillion Capital Finecosystem was founded on a universal belief that access to Capital is a right, not a privilege. Millions of people worldwide are denied loans, investment opportunities, and financial tools not because they lack ability, but because they don't have the Collateral for lending The Capital.

Exillion Capital Finecosystem introduces a breakthrough in DeFi Capital Lending through innovative Financial Engineering by introducing "mEXXE" as a Collateral Asset Token, a non-tradeble and non-transferable asset earned through time, discipline, responsibility and effort via mobile mining.

The Virtual Mining Token "mEXXE" is valued 1 : 1 to The SPL Token "EXXE".

Empowering Exillioneers to use The Virtual Mining Token "mEXXE" as Digital Collateral for lending The SPL Token "EXXE" on Solana Blockchain to receive The Capital with low interest rate — Exillioneers can use The Capital to invest and grow within a self-sustaining economic system in daily life.

The Angel Investor Funding Round open to everyone who believe DeFi Capital lending without traditional banking requirements — we are building a bridge and on-chain transparency to financial freedom for everyone

Let's join this innovative financial system where your time, discipline, responsibility and effort are truly valued.

Whitepaper Overview

01
Abstract & Vision
EXILLION Capital Finecosystem is built on universal belief — access to capital is a right, not a privilege. Millions are denied financial opportunity not due to lack of ability, but lack of collateral. EXILLION solves this through a self-sustaining DeFi lending finecosystem on Solana, where collateral is earned, not owned.
02
mEXXE Collateral Protocol
mEXXE is a non-tradeable, non-transferable Virtual Mining Token earned through daily discipline via the mEXXE mobile app. It serves as on-chain collateral — 1 mEXXE = 1 EXXE in borrowing value. Users lock mEXXE to access real capital with no bank, no credit score, and no traditional requirements.
03
Tokenomics & Supply
Fixed total supply of 11 Billion EXXE on Solana. Distributed across strategic pools: 55% (6.05B) Exillioneer Mining Pool, 10% (1.1B) Liquidity Pool, 7.5% (825M) Angel Investor Pool, 3% Treasury, 3% Community & Education, 3% Burn Reserve, and 1% (110M) Global Humanitarian Reserve — all governed on-chain.
04
Angel Investor Funding Round
825M EXXE is reserved exclusively for The Angel Investor Funding Round, open to everyone. Tier I (Genesis) at $0.01 — Tier II (Prime) at $0.025 — Tier III (Apex) at $0.05 — with Orca DEX listing target at $0.10. All funds are automatically distributed on-chain: 75% Liquidity, 15% Operational, 10% Development.
05
Solana Infrastructure & Transparency
EXILLION is a standard SPL token built on Solana — delivering 65,000 TPS throughput and sub-$0.001 transaction fees. Every fund movement is instantly distributed across three on-chain wallets and permanently verifiable on-chain. Full composability across the Solana DeFi and DEX via Orca.
06
Legal & Compliance
EXXE and mEXXE operate as utility tokens within the EXILLION finecosystem. Participation in the Angel Investor Round is voluntary and subject to individual jurisdiction compliance.

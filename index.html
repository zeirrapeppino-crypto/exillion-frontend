Whitepaper Overview

01 Abstract & Vision

EXILLION Capital Finecosystem is built on one universal belief — access to capital is a right, not a privilege. Millions are denied financial opportunity not due to lack of ability, but lack of collateral. EXILLION solves this through a self-sustaining DeFi lending ecosystem on Solana, where collateral is earned, not owned.

02 mEXXE Collateral Protocol

mEXXE is a non-tradeable, non-transferable Virtual Mining Token earned through daily discipline via the mEXXE mobile app. It serves as on-chain collateral — 1 mEXXE = 1 EXXE in borrowing value. Users lock mEXXE to access real capital with no bank, no credit score, and no traditional requirements.

03 Tokenomics & Supply

Fixed total supply of 11 Billion EXXE on Solana. Distributed across strategic pools: 55% (6.05B) Exillioneer Mining Pool, 10% (1.1B) Liquidity Pool, 7.5% (825M) Angel Investor Pool, 3% Treasury, 3% Community & Education, 3% Burn Reserve, and 1% (110M) Global Humanitarian Reserve — all governed on-chain.

04 Angel Investor Funding Round

825M EXXE is reserved exclusively for The Angel Investor Funding Round, open to everyone. Tier I (Genesis) at $0.01 — Tier II (Prime) at $0.025 — Tier III (Apex) at $0.05 — with Orca DEX listing target at $0.10. All funds are automatically distributed on-chain: 75% Liquidity, 15% Operational, 10% Development.

05 Solana Infrastructure & Transparency

EXILLION is a standard SPL token built on Solana — delivering 65,000 TPS throughput and sub-$0.001 transaction fees. Every fund movement is instantly distributed across three on-chain wallets and permanently verifiable on-chain. Full composability across the Solana DeFi and DEX ecosystem via Orca.

06 Legal & Compliance

EXXE and mEXXE operate as utility tokens within the EXILLION ecosystem.
